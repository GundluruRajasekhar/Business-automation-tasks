# Tasks

Mark a gate only after its evidence is in the plan notes.

## Week 1: Validate
- [ ] Write positioning sentence and interview guide (include "which tools have you tried and why did you stop?")
- [ ] List 30 prospects: interview contacts, competitor reviewers, seller communities
- [ ] Set up shared inbox, lead sheet, and question log
- **Gate:** 10 interviews booked

## Weeks 2-3: Interview and compare
- [ ] Complete 10-15 interviews; record posting frequency, tools, spend, exact wording
- [ ] Run the same 5 products through Predis, Ocoya, Canva, Buffer
- [ ] Measure real cost per post on 20-30 outputs
- **Gate:** 6 of 10 post weekly and call it a chore; 3 already pay; one gap confirmed by 4 sellers
- If the gate fails: test agencies or a different problem before building

## Weeks 3-4: Concierge pilot
- [ ] Make posts by hand for 5-10 sellers from their product links
- [ ] Track time per post, edit rate, and repeat requests
- [ ] Draft landing page with two price points; pick name after trademark and domain check
- **Gate:** half use 3+ posts in a week; 3+ ask to continue

## Weeks 5-6: Pre-sell and build smallest tool
- [ ] Offer a founding-member price; aim for 3-5 payments
- [ ] Build paste-a-URL page with prompt, template, copy or download
- [ ] Publish 2 guides and 1 fair comparison page
- **Gate:** 3-5 paying customers before adding features

## Later: Automate and scale
- [ ] Automate in this order: welcome email, weekly report, failure alerts, lead sheet, trial reminders
- [ ] Add AI support replies over approved help articles, with a human route
- [ ] Recheck unit costs, retention (months 2-3), and platform risk before paid growth
- **Gate:** each automation saves more time than it costs to maintain

## Open questions
- Cash available, founder pay, and country for registration costs
- Build myself, use no-code, or hire a freelancer
- Small sellers first, or agencies?
