# Requirements

## A. Plan tool (index.html)
- R1 Show all 14 stages as a flow with status colors.
- R2 Per stage: editable decision, open question, notes, status, and pass/fail criteria.
- R3 Show progress as stages passed out of 14.
- R4 Rank automations by score and let the user edit ratings.
- R5 Show a workflow map from signup to weekly report.
- R6 Save locally, export as JSON, reset on request.
- R7 Work on phones, with keyboard access, light and dark themes, and print.

## B. Product MVP (to validate first)
- P1 Input: a product URL or pasted product details (title, description, price, image).
- P2 Output: caption, hashtags, and an image layout for Instagram and Facebook. LinkedIn only if interviews show demand.
- P3 Seller can copy or download results. No accounts, dashboards, or auto-publishing in version 1.
- P4 Log every generation, seller edit, and cost per post.
- P5 Keep a manual fallback for every automated step.

## C. Constraints
- Check platform terms (Shopify, Etsy, Meta) and privacy and email rules for each market before collecting data or messaging.
- Verify competitor prices and AI provider prices before using them in decisions.
- Never publish invented quotes, statistics, or customer stories.

## D. Success measures
- Half of pilot sellers use 3+ posts in a week; most posts need only minor edits.
- 3+ sellers ask to continue, and 3-5 pay or pre-order.
- Cost per post fits inside the tested price.
